Why it matters:
🌊 Stateless Streamable HTTP (built for the 2026-07-28 MCP spec) → horizontal scaling with no sticky sessions
🔑 One Bearer token for all tools, fail-closed and timing-safe
🛡️ Secure by default: SSRF protection, READ-ONLY SQL, hardened HTTP surface, non-root containers
